Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 12 October 2026

1. Browsing

Noor Islamic is a static educational website. You can browse without creating an account.

2. Analytics

The main website and some content pages load Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-MGSVP7YZEF. Analytics may collect usage and device/browser information, page views, approximate location derived by Google and identifiers or cookies as described in Google’s partner-site privacy information.

3. Browser storage

The main website may save preferences or feature data, such as theme choices, saved items or counters, in local storage on your device. This is not a central account system; clearing browser data may remove saved items.

4. Location and external services

Location permission may be requested when you choose a location-based prayer-time feature. You may decline and manually select a city where available. Quran text, translations, audio, fonts and other features may be loaded from external providers, which may receive technical request information such as your IP address.

5. Contact form

If you submit a name, email and message, the information is intended to be used to respond. The main site’s code indicates email delivery depends on EmailJS configuration; if unavailable, a message may be stored in your browser instead of being sent. Do not include sensitive personal information or assume a message was delivered unless confirmed.

6. Advertising

If services such as Google AdSense are added in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. This policy must be reviewed and updated before ads are enabled, including any required disclosures and consent controls.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when features or providers change. Questions may be submitted through the contact page.

Noor Islamic is an independent educational website. Please verify religious text and references with reliable original sources.
