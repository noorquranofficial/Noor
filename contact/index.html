Contact

We welcome reports about possible errors, broken links, accessibility problems and source or licensing concerns.

What to include

  • The exact page URL.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • For religious-text corrections, a reference to a reliable original source.

Send a message

Open the contact section on the main Noor Islamic website and use its contact form if available.

Important: form email delivery depends on EmailJS configuration. If email is not configured, the form may save a message only in your browser; this does not send it to the site owner. Do not include sensitive personal information. Do not assume a message was received unless delivery is confirmed.
