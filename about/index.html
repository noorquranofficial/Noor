About

Noor (نور) means “light.” Noor Islamic brings together Quran study pages, selected Hadith, daily duas and related Islamic learning features in one place.

The website is an independent educational project. It is not affiliated with Google, a mosque, a publisher or a religious authority.

Our approach

  • Keep navigation clear and accessible on mobile and desktop.
  • Show available references and encourage checking important religious text against reliable original sources.
  • Be transparent about external services, sources and licensing limitations.

Corrections and feedback

If you notice a possible error, please use the contact section on the main Noor website. Include the page URL and a reliable source for the suggested correction.
